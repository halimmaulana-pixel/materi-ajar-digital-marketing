Course Overview

This course helps you understand how marketing works in digital channels and how Artificial Intelligence (AI) now shapes planning, content, advertising, measurement, and customer experience. You'll move from core concepts and strategy to hands-on tactics, analytics, marketing technology, and the legal-ethical rules that keep practice responsible. Every meeting includes learning objectives, plain-English explanations, detailed term definitions, tables and diagrams, a case study, exercises, and a real-world case with a detailed solution.

Language & Style
Clear English with every key term explained in detail. Writing is humanized—direct, concrete, and easy to follow.
Instruments
Consistent format across modules: term boxes, comparison tables, SVG charts/diagrams, case studies, and exercises.
AI Era Focus
AI is treated as a structural capability (not a side topic), with attention to discoverability, automation, personalization, and governance.
